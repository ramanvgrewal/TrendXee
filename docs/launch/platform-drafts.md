## Product Hunt

Name: **TrendXee**

Tagline: **Discover Indian indie fashion. Build your next fit.**

Short description:

Discover Indian indie fashion across nine lanes. Explore product galleries, browse brand information, save pieces and find complementary items with Pair up with. Shop directly at the retailer.

Maker comment:

Hi, I'm Raman, the maker of TrendXee.

I wanted one place to discover smaller Indian fashion labels and work out what to wear together, without losing the original store in a trail of tabs.

TrendXee organises pieces into nine lanes. Open a drop to explore the gallery and brand information, then use Pair up with to look at complementary items from other lanes. You can save pieces to an archive and buy through the retailer's site.

Instagram hype is shown when usable analytics are available. It is separate from ratings and does not guarantee product quality or service.

I'd love feedback on one task: find a piece you would wear and one item to pair with it. Where does that flow become confusing, and what information is missing before you'd buy?

## Hacker News

Title: **Show HN: TrendXee – Indian indie fashion discovery with outfit pairings**

URL: use the working homepage, not a blog article or sign-up screen.

Opening comment:

I built TrendXee to organise Indian fashion storefronts into a browsable catalogue and suggest complementary pieces across categories.

The stack is React with TanStack Start, Spring Boot APIs, and a separate local ingestion/AI worker. Pairings use product metadata and compatibility rules; they aren't generated by a fresh LLM call every time someone opens a product.

Some of the harder parts have been keeping images, titles and prices tied to the same product, handling inconsistent retailer data, and separating missing social analytics from low measured hype. Browsing does not require an account; saving and reviewing do.

I'd appreciate feedback on the browsing/pairing flow and how you would communicate data freshness and ranking uncertainty. Happy to discuss the architecture and trade-offs.

Use only implementation details you are comfortable discussing publicly. Do not post keys, operational logs, private data or quota details.
